Communication Workflows

Step-by-step workflows for the seven communication channels in the plan. Each channel starts from the flow written in the plan, then breaks it into steps with an owner, a trigger, the touchpoints used and the output that hands off to the next step.

    Reading each workflow

    The flow at the top of each channel is the chain written in the plan, word for word.

    The steps turn that chain into work. Each step lists who owns it, what starts it, the touchpoints used and what it produces for the next step.

    Objectives and KPIs answer the plan's requirement that every channel has clear, measurable objectives. Targets are left for the team to set against the budget model.

    Hands off to shows where one channel's output becomes another channel's input, so nothing is dropped between teams.

    Owners are proposed roles. Rename them to the people who hold each role.

    Built from vacario India, Fall 2026 Integrated Sales, Marketing and Promotions Plan, section “Communications Channels”. The channel flows are quoted from the plan; the steps, owners, cadences, KPIs and severity levels are a proposed operating version of it for the team to adjust.
