# Buddy the Rabbit: 15 brand videos for Google Flow

**Vacario™ · The Next Generation of Travel™**
Mascot video scripts built from the *V12.0 Vacario Brand Bible and Asset Catalogue*.

Every scene is 15 seconds and ties to a named section of the brand bible. Each one is two Google Flow (Veo) generations:

| Part | Length | How to make it in Flow |
|---|---|---|
| **Shot A** | 0–8 s | New clip: *Ingredients to Video* (with the Buddy reference image) or *Text to Video* |
| **Shot B** | 8–15 s | Select Shot A in the timeline → **Extend** → paste the Shot B prompt. Trim the result to 7 s. |
| **End card** | last ~1.5 s of Shot B | Add the logo **in your editor**, not in Flow (see rule 4 below) |

---

## Step 0: Create Buddy once, then reuse it

Veo keeps a character consistent best when you give it a reference image. Make one Buddy image first, save it as an **Ingredient** in Flow, and attach it to every Shot A.

**Image prompt (Flow → Create image / Imagen):**

```
Character reference sheet of "Buddy", a friendly 3D-animated rabbit mascot for a travel brand, high-end animated-feature-film style. Soft cream-white fur, tall upright ears with warm orange inner ears, big curious dark-brown eyes, small pink nose, gentle confident smile. Wears a sunset-orange (#EB8B30) zip hoodie, a leaf-green (#50752E) neck scarf, charcoal-grey (#252825) sneakers, and a small orange teardrop map-pin pendant with a green leaf on a cord around the neck. Small canvas backpack. Full body, front view and three-quarter view, standing on a plain white background, soft even studio light. No text, no logos.
```

Pick the best result and use it every time. Do not regenerate Buddy for each scene: that is where the look starts to drift.

### Why Buddy looks like this (brand bible links)

| Detail | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Orange hoodie and green scarf | Brand colours: Orange `#EB8B30`, Green `#50752E` (Colour and backgrounds, p.12) |
| Map-pin pendant with a leaf | The Vacario™ pin icon in the logo (Core logo lockups, p.9) |
| Charcoal sneakers | Charcoal `#252825`, the headline and text colour |
| Curious eyes, big ears that "pick up" hidden gems | Brand personality: **Authentic / Curious / Connected / Empowering** |
| Teal-blue and golden-orange colour grade | Approved widescreen gradient backgrounds (Digital asset catalogue, pp.58–59) |

### Buddy's personality (keep every line inside this)

- **Authentic:** points to something real, like a place, a person or a detail. Never hype without a subject.
- **Curious:** asks the brand's questions: *"Who will I meet?" "What will I discover?" "What experience will change me?"*
- **Connected:** credits locals, creators and fellow guests for the find. Buddy is a guide, not the hero.
- **Empowering:** ends by handing the moment to the viewer: *your* trip, *your* story.

## Brand rules used in every prompt

1. **Words.** Our community are **guests**. Never "users", "travellers" or "Gen Z'ers". Hotels, property owners, influencers and affiliates are **partners**. (How we speak, p.5)
2. **Gen Z phrases** only from the approved list, and only when they point to something real: *hidden gem, worth the hype, core memory, main character moment, travel dump, vibe check*. **"No cap"** is used only in a guest's or creator's own words, never by Buddy. Never use *NPC, mid, cooked, sus* or anything that sounds dismissive. (pp.10–11)
3. **Booking scenes use plain, direct language.** No slang near prices, terms or instructions, and no price or discount claims in these videos. (Where to use it, p.10)
4. **No logo or text inside the AI video.** Veo misspells text and redraws logos. Add the supplied logo artwork in your editor. Keep the pin colours as supplied: no outlines, shadows or gradients over the logo. (Artwork handling, p.12; "No icon, wordmark or positioning line may be embedded in these assets", p.58)
5. **End card.** Use the **Horizontal + statement** lockup (05) centred on a **white** panel, or **reverse white** on a charcoal panel or the approved gradient. Arial Bold for any extra line, sentence case. Write the brand as **Vacario™** and **The Next Generation of Travel™**.
6. **Footage look.** One clear subject, space for a headline, real places, people and details kept intact in the crop. (Photography, p.12)

Every prompt below ends with `No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.` Keep that line: it stops Veo burning in captions.

## Getting good results in Flow

- **Model:** use the Veo **Quality** model for anything with dialogue. Lip-sync and voice are noticeably better than on Fast.
- **Dialogue length:** keep each spoken line under about 18 words per 8-second shot. Longer lines get rushed or cut off. The lines above already fit.
- **Same voice every time:** keep the phrase *"warm, upbeat, friendly voice"* in every Shot A. If Buddy's voice drifts between scenes, record one voice-over line and replace the audio in your editor.
- **Consistency:** always attach the Step 0 Buddy image as an Ingredient for Shot A, and build Shot B with **Extend**, not as a new clip.
- **Crowds and places:** generated people and places are illustrative. Do not label a generated clip as a real location or a real guest's review.
- **Captions:** add them in your editor in Arial, sentence case, charcoal or white, never in the Flow prompt.
- **Regenerate rather than fix:** if Buddy's outfit changes colour, ears go wrong, or text appears in the frame, generate again. It is faster than editing it out.
