## Getting 30 s out of Flow
Veo makes about 8 seconds per generation, so Flow can't render 30 seconds in one go. To keep it one continuous take:

1. Paste the **master prompt** with the Buddy image attached. Flow generates the start of the take (0:00–0:08).
2. Click **Extend** and paste the **same master prompt**, adding this line at the very top:
   `Continue the same take from [0:08].`
3. Repeat with `[0:15]`, then `[0:23]`.
4. Flow joins the parts into **one 30-second video** with no cuts, because each Extend continues from the last frame.
