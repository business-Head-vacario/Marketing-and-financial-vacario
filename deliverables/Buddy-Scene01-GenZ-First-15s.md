# Buddy · Scene 01 · First 15 seconds (Gen Z cut)

**Film:** "Follow the Light" · **Time:** 0:00–0:15 · **Format:** 9:16 vertical (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)
**Brand link:** Our belief, *Go beyond the guidebook* (p.1). Gen Z language guide (How we speak, pp.10–12).

## What happens
A rainy day, a dusty guidebook, and Buddy is bored. Then the phone lights up with real stories, and the trip is on.

## Gen Z language used (all from the brand bible glossary)

| Phrase | Bible meaning | How it's used here |
|---|---|---|
| **POV** | "Introduces a perspective or scenario" (p.11) | Opening on-screen hook |
| **It's giving…** | "Describes a vibe or the impression something creates" (p.11) | Buddy roasting the old guidebook |
| **Low-key** | "Slightly or quietly" (p.11) | Buddy admitting what they need |
| **Touch grass** | "Step away from the internet and engage with the world around you" (p.11) | Buddy wants to get out into the real world |
| **We're so back** | Social and lifestyle expression (p.12) | The moment the phone lights up |

Tone check (p.11): the joke is aimed at an old guidebook, never at guests, partners or places. Buddy doesn't say "no cap": the bible keeps that for guests' and creators' own words.
