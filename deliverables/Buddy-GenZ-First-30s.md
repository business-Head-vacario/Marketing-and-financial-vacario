# Buddy · First 30 seconds (Gen Z cut)

**Film:** "Follow the Light" · **Time:** 0:00–0:30 (scenes 01 + 02) · **Format:** 9:16 vertical (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)
**Brand links:**

## The 30 seconds in one line
Bored on a rainy day with a dusty guidebook, Buddy's phone lights up with real people sharing real places right now. The question changes from "where should I stay?" to "who will I meet?", and Buddy is off.

| Time | Shot | Beat |

| 0:07.5–0:15 | B | Phone glows: "we're so back" |
| 0:15–0:22.5 | C | The light comes out of the phone and fills the room with real moments |
| 0:22.5–0:30 | D | "Who will I meet?" → "Say less." → chasing the light |

## How to build it in Flow (one chain)
1. Make **Buddy** (the character image from the main script) and **Backdrop B01**.
2. **Shot A:** *Ingredients to Video* with Buddy + B01.
3. **Shots B, C, D:** each one is **Extend** from the previous shot, so the room, light and Buddy stay continuous.
4. Trim each shot to 7.5 s, for **30 s** total.
5. If Shot C or D drifts off-model, regenerate it with **Jump to**, attaching Buddy + **Backdrop B02**.

## Shot A (0:00–0:07.5), Ingredients: Buddy + B01
```
Vertical 9:16, handheld phone-camera framing. Buddy, the friendly cream-white 3D-animated rabbit (high-end animated-film style) with orange inner ears, sunset-orange hoodie, leaf-green scarf, charcoal sneakers and a small orange map-pin pendant, flops dramatically backwards onto the window seat of this rainy apartment. Buddy holds up the dusty guidebook between two fingers, blows the dust off, gives the camera a deadpan side-eye and says in a warm, playful, friendly voice: "This guidebook? It's giving... ten years ago." Buddy drops it on a cushion, sighs, and adds: "Low-key need to touch grass. Somewhere new." Quick snap zoom on the side-eye. Sound: rain on glass, a soft comedic boop on the zoom. Cool grey-blue light. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Shot B (0:07.5–0:15), Extend
```
Continuing: the smartphone on the windowsill buzzes and lights up with a warm orange glow; faint sounds of waves, laughter and festival drums leak out of it. Buddy's ears shoot straight up, one at a time. Buddy slowly turns to the camera, eyes wide, and whispers: "Wait... we're so back." Fast snap zoom on Buddy's face, then Buddy grabs the glowing phone. End on the warm orange glow filling the bottom of the frame. Sound: rain fading out, notification chime, a beat drop starts. Cool grey light turning warm orange. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Shot C (0:15–0:22.5), Extend
```
Continuing, on the beat: Buddy holds up the glowing phone and a small glowing orange map-pin-shaped orb of light pops out of the screen and zips around the room. Wherever it passes, the grey walls flash to life with quick glimpses: waves on a hidden beach at sunrise, friends laughing in a courtyard café, lanterns at a night festival. The rain stops and the room turns warm gold. Buddy spins to follow it, ears bouncing, and says in a warm, playful, friendly voice: "Hold up... real people, sharing it live. A hidden gem from this morning? Obsessed." Fast whip-pans in rhythm with the beat. Sound: beat playing, whooshes, waves and laughter. Warm teal-blue and golden-orange light. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Shot D (0:22.5–0:30), Extend
```
Continuing: the glowing orange orb stops and hovers right in front of Buddy's face, pulsing gently as if waiting. Buddy leans into the camera with a knowing grin and says: "New question. Not 'where should I stay?'... who will I meet?" The orb pulses brighter, as if it answered. Buddy smirks: "Say less." Buddy grabs a small canvas backpack and swings it on as the orb streaks out of frame toward the door, and Buddy runs after it. End on the empty, glowing room with the orange light trail fading. Sound: beat builds and hits on "Say less", then a whoosh. Warm golden light. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```
