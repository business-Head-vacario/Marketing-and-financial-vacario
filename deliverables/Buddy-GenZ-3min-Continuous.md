# Buddy · "Follow the Light" · the full 3 minutes (Gen Z, continuous)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** 0:00–3:00 · one continuous film with no hard cuts
**Built on:** the *V12.0 Vacario Brand Bible*. Every slang phrase comes from its Gen Z guide (pp.10–12). **Vacario™ · The Next Generation of Travel™**

## How this works
The film is **one continuous take**. It's written as **six chapters of 30 seconds** only so each master prompt stays short enough for Flow to follow. There are no cuts between chapters: each one starts on the exact frame where the last one ends.

**In Flow:**
1. **Chapter 1, first part:** paste Master Prompt 1 with the **Buddy image** attached.
2. **Every part after that:** click **Extend**, paste the **current chapter's master prompt**, and add this line at the top: `Continue the same take from [time].` Use these times in order:
   **0:08, 0:15, 0:23**, then **0:30, 0:38, 0:45, 0:53, 1:00, 1:08, 1:15, 1:23, 1:30, 1:38, 1:45, 1:53, 2:00, 2:08, 2:15, 2:23, 2:30, 2:38, 2:45, 2:53**.
   When the time reaches a new chapter (0:30, 1:00, 1:30, 2:00, 2:30), switch to that chapter's master prompt.
3. Flow chains every part into **one 3-minute video**, because each Extend continues from the last frame.
4. If a part drifts off-model (wrong outfit or face), redo it with **Jump to**, attaching Buddy plus that chapter's **backup backdrop**.

**Rules for every chapter:**
- Buddy speaks in the same *warm, playful, friendly voice*.
- The Buddy description is repeated word for word in every master prompt.
- No on-screen text, subtitles or logos are generated in Flow. Text and the logo go in the editor.

**Master Prompt 1**
```
Vertical 9:16, one continuous handheld phone-camera take with no cuts. Buddy, the friendly cream-white 3D-animated rabbit (high-end animated-film style) with orange inner ears, sunset-orange hoodie, leaf-green scarf, charcoal sneakers and a small orange map-pin pendant, in a cosy small city apartment on a grey rainy afternoon, with a window seat, rain on the glass and a dusty old travel guidebook. [0:00–0:07] Cool grey-blue light, rain on the glass. Buddy flops dramatically backwards onto the window seat, holds up the dusty guidebook between two fingers, blows the dust off, gives the camera a deadpan side-eye: "This guidebook? It's giving... ten years ago." Drops it on a cushion, sighs: "Low-key need to touch grass. Somewhere new." Quick snap zoom on the side-eye.

[0:07–0:15] The smartphone on the windowsill buzzes and glows warm orange; faint waves, laughter and festival drums leak out. Buddy's ears shoot up one at a time. Buddy slowly turns to camera, eyes wide, and whispers: "Wait... we're so back." Snap zoom; Buddy grabs the glowing phone. A beat drops.

[0:15–0:23] On the beat, a small glowing orange map-pin-shaped orb of light pops out of the phone and zips around the room. Wherever it passes, the walls flash with glimpses of a hidden beach at sunrise, friends laughing in a courtyard café and lanterns at a night festival. The rain stops and the room turns warm gold. Buddy spins after it, ears bouncing: "Hold up... real people, sharing it live. A hidden gem from this morning? Obsessed."

[0:23–0:30] The orb hovers in front of Buddy's face, pulsing. Buddy leans into the camera with a knowing grin: "New question. Not 'where should I stay?'... who will I meet?" The orb pulses brighter. Buddy smirks: "Say less." Buddy flops onto the sofa, phone in hand, as the orb hovers above it.

Sound: rain, comedic boop, notification chime, beat drop on "we're so back", whooshes. Colour: cool grey-blue turning warm teal-blue and golden orange. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```
