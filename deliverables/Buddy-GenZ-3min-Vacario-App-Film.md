# Buddy · "Inside the App" · 3-minute Vacario™ app film (Gen Z, continuous)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** 0:00–3:00 · one continuous take with no hard cuts
**What it's about:** the Vacario™ app itself. Buddy jumps inside the phone and tours the app's **five tabs: Explore, Plan, Book, Community, Trips**, then steps back out to live the trip and share it.
**Built on:** the *V12.0 Vacario Brand Bible*:
- Digital design standards (pp.23–32): the five app tabs, AI planning, booking, community
- Brand promise (p.2), positioning (p.4)
- Gen Z guide (pp.10–12)

---

## The idea
> "Give guests a place to **discover** a trip, **shape it with AI**, **book with confidence** and **share what they find**." (Digital look and feel, p.23)

Bored at a rainy window, Buddy gets pulled *into* the Vacario™ app by the glowing orange pin of light. Inside, the app is a bright, living world:

| Zone | What Buddy finds |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | A city of live stories from real guests, creators and locals |
| **Plan** | An AI that builds a trip from one sentence |
| **Book** | Clear prices and terms, booked in one place |
| **Community** | Circles, group trips and the people Buddy will meet |
| **Trips** | Everything saved in one place |

Buddy steps out into the real trip, then shares a story back into the app, and that story becomes someone else's reason to go.

## The look inside the app (keep it on-brand)
Use this description for the "inside the app" world in every chapter. It's taken from the brand bible's digital standards (pp.27–29):
- **Light core shell:** a bright white and soft-field (`#F1F3EE`) world with floating rounded cards (12–16 radius), and **deep leaf `#355323`** glowing buttons.
- **Orange `#EB8B30`** only for small accents and the guiding pin of light. **Charcoal `#252825`** for anything dark.
- **9:16 video cards** float like windows into real places (the bible uses 9:16 for short video, p.29).
- Five floating zones connected by soft paths: **Explore, Plan, Book, Community, Trips**.
- **No readable text, icons or UI labels are generated in Flow.** AI garbles text. Add the tab names in the editor and, for credibility, cut in **real screen recordings of the Vacario™ app** at the moments marked 📱.

# CHAPTER 1 · 0:00–0:30 · Pulled into the app

**Master Prompt 1**
```
Vertical 9:16, one continuous handheld phone-camera take with no cuts. Buddy, the friendly cream-white 3D-animated rabbit (high-end animated-film style) with orange inner ears, sunset-orange hoodie, leaf-green scarf, charcoal sneakers and a small orange map-pin pendant, in a cosy small city apartment on a grey rainy afternoon, with a window seat, rain on the glass and a dusty old travel guidebook. Buddy speaks in a warm, playful, friendly voice throughout.

[0:00–0:07] Cool grey-blue light. Buddy flops backwards onto the window seat, holds up the dusty guidebook between two fingers, blows off the dust and gives the camera a deadpan side-eye: "This guidebook? It's giving... ten years ago." Drops it, sighs: "Low-key need to touch grass. Somewhere new." Snap zoom on the side-eye.

[0:07–0:15] The smartphone on the windowsill buzzes and glows warm orange; faint waves, laughter and drums leak out. Buddy's ears shoot up one at a time. Buddy turns to camera, eyes wide, and whispers: "Wait... we're so back." Buddy grabs the phone. A beat drops.

[0:15–0:23] A small glowing orange map-pin-shaped orb of light pops out of the screen, circles Buddy, then tugs gently at Buddy's scarf toward the phone. Buddy leans closer to the screen, curious: "Okay, okay... show me." The phone screen stretches open like a glowing doorway, wide enough to step through.

[0:23–0:30] Buddy shrinks down and hops through the glowing screen. The camera follows in one move, through a rush of soft orange and green light, into a bright, airy world inside the app: clean white and pale-green space, floating rounded cards, glowing paths to five floating zones. Buddy lands, looks around in awe: "Hold on... I'm inside the app?"

Sound: rain, comedic boop, notification chime, beat drop on "we're so back", a whoosh through the screen, then airy, bright music. Colour: cool grey-blue turning to a bright white and pale-green world with orange accents. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```
