For Google Flow (Veo + Imagen). Built on the *V12.0 Vacario Brand Bible*. **Vacario™ · The Next Generation of Travel™**

This brings every idea from the earlier scripts into one 3-minute film:
- the guidebook opening
- the guiding light
- "Who will I meet?"
- AI planning and booking
- a partner host's welcome
- hidden gem, vibe check, travel dump, worth the hype, main character moment and core memory
- the live festival tip
- the story that inspires someone else to go

A timed **animatic** (`Buddy-3min-Animatic.mp4`) comes with this script. It shows every scene's backdrop, timing and dialogue, so you can review the pacing before you generate anything.

## How to make it in Google Flow

### Step 1: Make Buddy (one image, used in every scene)
```
Character reference sheet of "Buddy", a friendly 3D-animated rabbit mascot for a travel brand, high-end animated-feature-film style. Soft cream-white fur, tall upright ears with warm orange inner ears, big curious dark-brown eyes, small pink nose, gentle confident smile. Wears a sunset-orange (#EB8B30) zip hoodie, a leaf-green (#50752E) neck scarf, charcoal-grey (#252825) sneakers, a small orange teardrop map-pin pendant with a green leaf on a cord, and a small canvas backpack. Full body, front and three-quarter views, plain white background, soft studio light. No text, no logos.
```

### Step 2: Make the 12 backdrops
Use **Flow → Create image** with the backdrop prompts below. Backdrops are empty sets, with **no characters and no text**, so Veo has a fixed place to stage Buddy in. They all share the same colour grade (deep teal-blue and golden orange, taken from the brand's gradient backgrounds, pp.58–59), so the 12 scenes look like one film.

### Step 3: Generate each scene
| Part | Length | In Flow |
|---|---|---|
| **Shot A** | 7.5 s | *Ingredients to Video*: attach **Buddy** + **that scene's backdrop**, then paste the Shot A prompt |
| **Shot B** | 7.5 s | Select Shot A → **Extend** → paste the Shot B prompt |

Trim each shot to 7.5 s. 12 scenes × 15 s = **3:00**.

### Step 4: Assemble
- Put the scenes in order in Scenebuilder or your editor. Each scene ends on the moment the next one starts (see "Transition").
- Lay **one continuous music track** under the whole film and turn the Veo music down. Keep the dialogue and sound effects.
- Add the **logo end card (2:57–3:00) and any captions in the editor only.** Never put the logo in a Flow prompt (Artwork handling, p.12; "No icon, wordmark or positioning line may be embedded in these assets", p.58).
- Every video prompt ends with `No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.` Keep it.
