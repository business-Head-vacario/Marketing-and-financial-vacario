# "Follow the Light": a single Buddy film for Vacario™

**One continuous video · 3 minutes 45 seconds · cut into 15 clips of 15 seconds for Google Flow**
Built on the *V12.0 Vacario Brand Bible*. **Vacario™ · The Next Generation of Travel™**

---

## What holds it together as one video

Three threads run through the whole film, so it reads as one film rather than 15 separate clips:

1. **The guiding light.** A small **glowing orange map-pin of light**, inspired by the Vacario™ pin, comes out of Buddy's phone in clip 01. It stands for *stories shared by real guests, creators and locals*. Buddy follows it through every clip, and almost every clip ends with the light leading into the next one.
2. **One continuous camera.** No clip starts fresh. Every clip **begins on exactly the frame the previous clip ended on** (see "Starts on" and "Ends on" in each clip). In Flow, the whole film is built as one chain of **Extend** steps.
3. **One loop.** The film opens with Buddy bored at a rainy window and ends with *someone else* at a rainy window receiving Buddy's story. That loop is the brand promise: *"return with a story that inspires someone else to go"* (Brand story, p.2).

# PART 1: The script, read as one film

> Read this top to bottom like a short film. Part 2 cuts the same script into 15-second clips.

**[00:00]** A grey, rainy afternoon. A small city apartment. BUDDY, a cream-white rabbit in an orange hoodie and green scarf, slumps at the window flipping through a dusty guidebook.
**BUDDY:** "Same guidebook. Same photos. Same 'top ten'..."
Buddy shuts the book. Dust puffs. Rain on the glass.
**BUDDY:** "There's got to be more to the world than this."
The phone on the sill buzzes and glows warm orange.

**[00:15]** Buddy picks it up. A small glowing orange pin of light rises out of the screen and circles the room. Wherever it passes, the walls fill with sound and colour: waves on a hidden beach, laughter in a café, distant drums.
**BUDDY:** "Wait... these are real people. Sharing it right now."
The light hovers in front of Buddy's face, waiting.
**BUDDY:** "So it's not 'where should I stay?' ...it's 'who will I meet?'"

**[00:30]** The light spins out into ribbons that form a living plan in the air: backwaters, tea hills, a lantern festival, a beach at sunrise.
**BUDDY:** "A relaxed four-day trip to Kerala."
Buddy taps the ribbons into a neat row, then presses one glowing button. *Click.*
**BUDDY:** "Stay and experiences, booked in one place."

**[00:45]** A quick pack: shirt, sunglasses, sandals, backpack zipped. The light darts to the front door. Buddy runs after it, and the door opens straight onto an aeroplane window high above the clouds.
**BUDDY:** "Kerala... here I come!"
The light slips through the glass and dives toward the green coast below.

**[01:00]** The camera dives with it, through the clouds, over palm-lined rivers, down to a leafy lane in Fort Kochi. The light settles on the gate of a red-roofed family homestay. Buddy arrives, out of breath. The HOST, a warm Malayali woman, opens the gate.
**HOST:** "Welcome! Come, you must be hungry."
**BUDDY:** "Always!"

**[01:15]** On the veranda: a banana-leaf lunch with the whole family. Buddy takes a bite and closes both eyes.
**BUDDY:** "Okay... I already feel at home."
**HOST** (leaning in, pointing): "Tomorrow? Turn left at the blue door."
Outside the gate, the light blinks, as if it heard.

**[01:30]** Buddy follows the light down a lane of spice shops and art walls to a faded blue door, and into a hidden courtyard café under an old mango tree. A barista pours filter coffee from a height.
**BUDDY** (sipping): "Hidden gem. Officially."
The light drifts up through the leaves toward the sky.

**[01:45]** The sky time-lapses to the next sunrise over the sea. Buddy stretches on a red clifftop above a quiet beach.
**BUDDY:** "Vibe check. Slow morning... ocean air..."
A few steps to a tiny café. A fresh coconut.
**BUDDY:** "...and a café I can walk to. Passed."
Dark monsoon clouds roll in over the sea.

**[02:00]** Rain. A travel dump in quick snaps: Buddy under an umbrella, laughing; a roadside vendor pouring chai from a height; Buddy blowing on the glass.
**BUDDY:** "Day three travel dump. Rain... roadside chai..."
The rain stops. The light reappears and leads Buddy down a muddy path to a huge waterfall with a rainbow.
**BUDDY:** "...and one very good detour."

**[02:15]** At dusk, Buddy's phone buzzes. Drums come out of it.
**BUDDY:** "Someone just posted this. Tonight. Ten minutes away."
The light zips off toward a glow behind the palms. Buddy runs after it.
**BUDDY:** "Oh, I can hear it from here!"

**[02:30]** A village festival: oil lamps, chenda drummers, marigolds, sparks in the sky. A YOUNG LOCAL holds out a hand.
**LOCAL:** "Come, dance with us!"
Buddy joins the circle, ears flopping, laughing.
**BUDDY:** "Okay... I'm not just visiting anymore."
The light bursts into sparks above the crowd, and the drumbeat turns into an engine.

**[02:45]** Morning. The open back of a jeep climbing through endless tea hills in Munnar. Wind in Buddy's ears and scarf.
**BUDDY:** "Windows down. Hills ahead."
A huge valley opens up. Buddy stands, arms out.
**BUDDY:** "Main character moment. No skipping this one."
The light races ahead, up toward a dark peak.

**[03:00]** Pre-dawn on the peak. Buddy sits beside a young CREATOR with a camera.
**BUDDY** (whispering): "Everyone says this sunrise is worth the hype..."
The sun bursts over a sea of clouds.
**CREATOR:** "No cap, worth the 4 a.m. alarm."
**BUDDY:** "Okay. I see it now."

**[03:15]** The sun sweeps across the sky and sets over the backwaters. A lantern-lit houseboat. Around the table: the host, the local from the festival, the creator and new friends.
**BUDDY:** "Four days ago, I didn't know any of you."
Stars come out. Singing. Buddy raises the phone for a group photo. *Flash.*
**BUDDY:** "Core memory unlocked."

**[03:30]** The flash fades to the same apartment from the opening, now sunny. The guidebook sits under a small plant. Buddy taps *share*. A new orange light rises and flies out of the window.
**BUDDY:** "I found this trip through someone's story. Now it's my turn."
Across the city, it lands on the phone of a young woman at a rainy window, exactly where Buddy started. She smiles.
**BUDDY** (to camera): "This is the next generation of travel. Come be part of it."
**[03:41]** End card: Vacario™ · The Next Generation of Travel™ · vacario.ai

# PART 2: The same script, cut into 15 clips of 15 seconds

## How to build it in Google Flow (one chain)

**Step 0: Make Buddy once** (Flow → Create image), then save the image as an **Ingredient**:
```
Character reference sheet of "Buddy", a friendly 3D-animated rabbit mascot for a travel brand, high-end animated-feature-film style. Soft cream-white fur, tall upright ears with warm orange inner ears, big curious dark-brown eyes, small pink nose, gentle confident smile. Wears a sunset-orange (#EB8B30) zip hoodie, a leaf-green (#50752E) neck scarf, charcoal-grey (#252825) sneakers, a small orange teardrop map-pin pendant with a green leaf on a cord, and a small canvas backpack. Full body, front and three-quarter views, plain white background, soft studio light. No text, no logos.
```

**Then build the film as one chain in Scenebuilder:**

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| Clip 01, Part A | *Ingredients to Video* with the Buddy image. The only fresh generation in the whole film. |
| Every part after that | Select the last clip in the timeline → **Extend** → paste the next prompt. Flow continues from the final frame, so the film never resets. |
| If an Extend drifts | Use **Jump to** with the Buddy Ingredient attached, and paste the same prompt. |
| Length | Each part is about 8 s. Trim each to **7.5 s**: two parts = 15 s per clip, 30 parts = 3:45. |

**Rules for the whole film:**
- Keep *"warm, upbeat, friendly voice"* in every prompt where Buddy speaks.
- Keep the last line of every prompt: `No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.`
- Add the **logo, captions and one continuous music bed** only in the editor. The logo must not be generated (Artwork handling, p.12; asset rules, p.58).
- Buddy's outfit never changes. If a part changes it, regenerate that part.
